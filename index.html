About Us

Built by people who
understand your work.

Sumext was born from watching accountants drown in manual data entry — typing the same vendor names and tax codes thousands of times a week, working late to close books, and turning away clients because their team couldn't scale.

We believe the future of accounting isn't about replacing accountants with AI. It's about giving accountants their time back so they can do what they do best — advise clients, catch risks, and build relationships that technology could never replace.

FAQ

Common questions

What happens after my 14-day trial? +
After 14 days you'll be prompted to choose a paid plan. If you don't upgrade, your account switches to read-only — you can still view past documents but cannot process new ones. No charges until you choose a plan.
Can I switch plans anytime? +
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. When you upgrade, you're charged the prorated difference immediately. When you downgrade, the change takes effect at your next billing cycle.
What counts as a document? +
Each file upload counts as one document regardless of page count. A 10-page invoice is still 1 document. Multi-page bank statements count as 1 document. Only successful extractions count towards your monthly limit.
How do contractor seats work on Team? +
Free trial includes 3 contractor seats so you can test the full workflow before paying. Solo plan allows contractors as an add-on at $5/seat/month. Team plan includes 5 contractor seats. Each seat lets one subcontractor, field worker, or freelancer submit invoices and timesheets directly through the Sumext portal. You can add more contractor seats for $5/seat/month beyond the included amount.
Which accounting software is supported? +
Solo supports Xero and QuickBooks. Team and Firm additionally support Zoho Books and Tally. Firm also supports MYOB. All plans support CSV export for any accounting software not listed. More integrations coming in 2026.
Is my data secure? +
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256). We never use your financial documents to train AI models. You can export or delete all your data at any time. We are working towards ISO 27001 certification.
What if I process more than my plan limit? +
You'll get an email warning at 80% usage. At 100% you can either upgrade your plan or purchase a top-up pack (100 extra documents for $4). Your existing documents are never deleted.
Do you offer annual billing? +
Yes — toggle to annual billing above and save 20% across all plans. Annual billing is charged once per year. Solo annual: $182. Team annual: $470. Firm annual: $950. You can switch back to monthly at renewal.
